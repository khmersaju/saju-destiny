Privacy Policy

Saju Destiny (operated by HEBRON ACADEMY) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our app.

Effective Date: May 22, 2025 | Last Updated: May 22, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Account registration: name, email address, phone number, date of birth, gender, city
  • Fortune reading inputs: birth year, month, day, hour, and gender used for Saju calculations

Information collected automatically:

  • App usage data: features accessed, fortune reading history, session duration
  • Device information: device type, operating system version (used for crash reporting and compatibility only)
  • App click/visit counters (anonymous, not linked to personal identity)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide personalized Saju (Four Pillars) fortune readings based on your birth data
  • To maintain your account and remember your preferences
  • To improve app features, fix bugs, and enhance user experience
  • To send optional notifications (only if you explicitly opt in)
  • To display aggregate usage statistics (e.g., total app clicks — no personal data shown)

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We do not share your data with third parties except:

  • As required by applicable law or legal process
  • To protect the rights and safety of our users
  • With AI service providers (OpenAI) solely to generate fortune reading text — only anonymized birth data (year, month, day, hour, gender) is sent; no name or contact information is transmitted

4. Data Storage & Retention

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Fortune reading history is retained for up to 12 months. You may request deletion at any time.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal information, we will delete it immediately.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Withdraw consent for optional features (e.g., notifications)
